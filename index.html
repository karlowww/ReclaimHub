Company Description

ReclaimHub is a school-based Lost and Found Management System designed to make reporting, tracking, and recovering lost items easier and more organized. The system provides a centralized digital platform where students and administrators can record lost or found belongings and monitor their status.

Nature of the Organization

ReclaimHub is an educational technology project focused on improving school property recovery through a simple web-based information management system.
